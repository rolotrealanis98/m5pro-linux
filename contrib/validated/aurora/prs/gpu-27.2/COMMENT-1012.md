The macOS 27.2 and 27.3 GPU firmware support for the M5 Pro is in a separate PR stacked on this branch: #PR_NUMBER. It adds 13 commits on b1615e3fca90 and none of #1012's commits change. The 27.2+ firmware checks a different InitData graph: the root version word gains bit 46, the power policy grows to 0x12728 bytes, the status config to 0x790, and 27.3 beta 1 inserts a HW data word at 0x2720. The PR builds that graph and selects it by firmware identity. It also adds J716s bare-metal tables and the G17S firmware identities for 27.0, 27.2 and 27.3 beta 1. On a J716s with the 27.3 beta 1 firmware, both InitData are acknowledged, the opening records are retired, and `[drm] Initialized aurora 1.0.0` follows with no faults.

For 26A428 builds, which are all the M5 Max and M5 Ultra identify, every new map is the identity. Standalone checks compile the driver's encoders with rustc and confirm the 27.0 graph is byte-identical to this branch's. The PR body lists them, along with what is inferred rather than measured. The 27.2 and 27.3 beta 1 digests for G17C and G17D are in the PR too, if you want those boards on 27.2+. A separate PR will cover the firmware-27.3 display method table.
