J716s (MacBook Pro 16-inch, M5 Pro) update, all on Aurora's `integration/aurora` 7.3 plus the T6050 PRs, tested on the machine today. Everything is in https://github.com/rolotrealanis98/m5pro-linux; evidence in `contrib/validated/integration/RESULTS.md`.

- **WFI idle:** m1n1 appends `idle=nop arm64.nowfxt`, so the J716s never idled; the cores just spun. A kernel override for the T6050 MIDRs (same as 5c9cbb25dba8 for T8142, opt-out `apple_keep_idle_nop`) takes idle on the charger from 16.4 W to 9.8 W. 56 min soak clean, FP check on all 18 CPUs. Patch: `contrib/pending/wfi-idle/`; PR aurora-silicon/linux#1152.
- **Sleep:** s2idle S1-S4 pass (`pm_test` freezer/devices/platform, then a real `mem` sleep woken by the power button). Lid close used to wake 0.7 s later: the SMC itself sends a lid-open (`03 00 01`) about 230 ms into the suspend. macsmc-input now checks `MSLD` before waking and ignores an open in the first 2 s (`lid_wake_settle_ms`). Lid close sleeps and lid open wakes; it is in my daily kernel. `contrib/pending/platform/lid-sleep/`.
- **SMC sensors and right port:** `macsmc_hwmon` with both fans, 60+ temperatures and the power rails (`contrib/pending/dt-prep/patches/hwmon/`). The right Type-C port in host mode: the hub enumerates at 10 Gb/s, and the a1 SPMI bus with its `sn201202x` works (`dt-prep/patches/right-port/`).
- **Speakers:** they play clean, with no `bad TDM clock`. The J716 speaker link needs the J616s TDM format (normal bit clock, inverted frame clock, 250-bit frame), as the J716s ADT's serializer describes. With the J714 settings, all six amps faulted for the whole stream. `contrib/pending/audio/aurora/tdm/patches/0001-0004`.
- **Wi-Fi and Bluetooth:** both up. WPA2 5 GHz association, DHCP, 17 ms pings; `hci0` sees 36 devices. Recipe:
  - m1n1 trains apcie0 port 0, which iBoot leaves untrained: `contrib/pending/wireless/patches/m1n1/0022-0026`, PR aurora-silicon/m1n1#65. It starts ANS for the storage check, pulses SMC `gP13` as on the J813, gets Gen1 x1 and `106b:1900`.
  - FTAB: the bundle `ftab.bin` has no ticket, and the ROM refuses it (`phase0: -5`). Stitch `centauri.j716sap.im4m` from the macOS restore bundle into the ticket slot with `contrib/pending/wireless/aurora-n1/w3/mk-ftab-w3.py`. The manifest is per board and static: no ECID, no nonce, all 38 digests match. Put the result in the initramfs, since `APPLE_CENTAURI=y` reads it from there.
  - ASPM must stay off on the adopted port. Without `pcie_aspm=off`, a cold boot froze about 30 s after login, as on your J714c. I have a pcie-apple patch that strips L0s/L1 on adopted root ports, still to be booted.
  - Not solved yet: a restart with the N1 link alive does not come back (iBoot counts a failed boot), so this stays a test payload for now.
