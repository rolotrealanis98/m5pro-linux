Watch v4 and the avd-fw repo for a T8142 image; when upstream grows `apple,t8142-avd`, our `"apple,t6050-avd", "apple,t8142-avd"` pair already has the right shape.

**What Asahi posted.** "Initial USB4/Thunderbolt support for Apple M1/M2/M3 SoCs", Sven Peter: v1 2026-08-30 (19 patches, `20260830-b4-apple-soc-tbt-v1-0-44bc9348683c@kernel.org`), **v2 2026-09-06 (22 patches, `20260906-b4-apple-soc-tbt-v2-0-1f80085f93fb@kernel.org`**; mirror https://lkml.iu.edu/hypermail/linux/kernel/2609.0/13926.html; binding .../13923.html; t60xx DTS .../13944.html). **What we carry.** The daily (`/Volumes/linux-cs/linux` `j716s-daily-n1k`) contains Aurora's import of Sven's series at its 2026-08-30 shape (`2c013cba44e2` "thunderbolt: Add Apple Silicon support", `9dc86285e55c` CIO reset, `QUIRK_NO_DMA_PORT`, `thunderbolt-switch` still in the binding and in `t6050-die0.dtsi:460`), Aurora's T8132 and T6050 routers (`c3dee268ed8b`/`d50de631e0a1`, Erik Bourget, "Developed with Claude Code": Type 7 routers with an MxWrap wrapper, CIO reconfig at PMGR page offset 0x60, LSTX handover once RTKit is up, Gen4 cable word, SRAM parity IRQ), DjDeveloperr's PCIe-C and DP-IN work (`apple-dpin-*.c`), plus our 16 `contrib/pending/thunderbolt/patches/daily-stack/` patches and the 15 `patches/kernel/` DT/binding patches (right port ATC2 only; never ATC0, the tether). `apple.c` matches `apple,t8103/t6020/t6030/t8132/t8142-usb4-acio` and the T6050 entries. Status: "Attended only (T0 hung)" (README row 58); analysis `contrib/pending/thunderbolt/T0-HANG-ANALYSIS.md`.

**Why ours hung and theirs does not (as far as static analysis goes).** T0's kernel delta is Sven's core + Aurora's T6050 layer, but the DTB delta added 10 pmgr power-controller nodes that `PD_FLAG_ATTACH_POWER_ON` switches on at probe, powering `atc2_common/atc2_cio/atc1_common/atc1_cio` "with the PHY not in CIO mode, which apple.c:3523-3531 says must not happen", and `*_cio_auss` deferring forever. That is exactly the ordering constraint Sven's cover letter and binding spell out (ACIO powers only after the PHY is in USB4 mode, driven by the Type-C notifier). The fix already written in the analysis (acio2, its mailbox and the `*_cio_auss` domains `status = "disabled"` in `t6050-max.dtsi`, enabled per board with their suppliers) is the upstream shape (`t600x-dieX.dtsi` nodes disabled, boards enable). The remaining T0 candidates (kernel/config delta independent of the DT; mailboxes) are what the K0/D1/D2/D3 ladder separates.

**T6050 / Thunderbolt 5.** Not in the series, and the series' binding (`t8103` fallback for every SoC) does not fit the T6050 routers, which need their own start sequence; Aurora's `apple,t6050-usb4-acio` without a t8103 fallback is right. PCIe and DP tunnels are not upstream for any SoC; our 0013-0016 PCIe-C patches are Aurora-only and stay so. Suspend with an active tunnel is unsupported upstream; our `lid-sleep` tests must keep TB unplugged.

**Carry.** Aurora's stack as is (it is Sven's series plus the T6050 layer; replacing it with upstream v2 gains nothing for T6050). Prepare for v2's DT contract when Aurora rebases: drop `thunderbolt-switch`, move the connector endpoint to `port@2`, add the dwc3 `port@1/endpoint@1` link, keep `usb4-N-acio`/`usb4-N-nhi` aliases (also in v2). Run the ladder attended (K0 first; never T1 before K0/D1 pass; `watchdog.open_timeout=300` on every test payload).
